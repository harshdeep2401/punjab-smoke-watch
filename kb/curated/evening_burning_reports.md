# Reporting from November 2024

The Tribune (7 November 2024) reported that farmers in Punjab were burning stubble in the evening and at night to avoid satellite detection. The article quotes Dr Vinay Sehgal, principal scientist at the CREAMS laboratory in Delhi, who said the satellites cross the region in the afternoon and well after midnight, that farmers had come to know this, that evening and night fires were rising, and that this had led to a decline in cases on the crop residue burning dashboard.

The same article quotes Kahan Singh Pannu, a former chairman of the Punjab Pollution Control Board, saying it cannot be ruled out that farm fire incidents have declined because of the efforts of successive governments.

This is press reporting and expert opinion. The article gives no counts of the fires that were missed.
