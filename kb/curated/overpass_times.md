# Pass times of the fire satellites

NASA's FIRMS FAQ gives these times at the equator, in mean local time. Terra (MODIS) passes at about 10:30 in the morning and 10:30 at night. Aqua (MODIS) passes at about 1:30 in the afternoon and 1:30 at night. Suomi NPP (VIIRS) passes at about 13:30 and 01:30. The FAQ says NOAA-20 and NOAA-21 (VIIRS) run about 50 minutes ahead of Suomi NPP.

The FAQ also says these satellites only detect fires that are active at the time of an overpass. A fire that starts and ends between passes is not recorded.

# The satellites are not fully independent looks

The three VIIRS satellites all see Punjab around the early afternoon and after midnight. In the data downloaded for this project their typical day-pass times over the region were within about half an hour of each other. Agreement between them shows that a change is not one instrument's fault. It does not show what happened in the late afternoon or evening.

# Orbit drift

Terra made its last orbit-holding manoeuvre in February 2020 and Aqua in March 2021. Since then Terra crosses earlier and Aqua later (source: https://modis-land.gsfc.nasa.gov/news.html). So a change in MODIS fire counts between years is partly a change in the time of observation.
