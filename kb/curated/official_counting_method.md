# What an official count is

The Punjab Pollution Control Board's action taken report for the 2024 season lists, district by district, the number of fire incidents reported by the Punjab Remote Sensing Centre (PRSC) up to 30 November 2024. These are satellite detections. The district tables used for 2022 and 2023 are also PRSC satellite data, for 15 September to 30 November.

Because the official count is satellite-based, it shares the blind spots of satellite fire detection, including the timing of the passes. Agreement between an official count and the NASA detections used here is therefore not an independent confirmation.

# What the field visits do and do not show

The 2024 report says teams visited 10,791 of the 10,909 reported incidents, and that at 5,090 sites no crop burning was observed, marked as still under verification. Field visits check places where a fire was detected. They say nothing about burning that no satellite detected.
